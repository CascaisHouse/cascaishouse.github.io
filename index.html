The house

This is a private two-story home. From the street, you step into a stone entryway that opens onto a bright foyer. The rest of the ground floor holds a guest bathroom, the kitchen, and an open-plan living and dining room that leads out to the deck, garden, and barbecue area. Off the dining area is a music nook with a writer's desk, a piano, and a guitar.

Upstairs, the main bedroom has its own walk-in closet and its own bathroom. A second, furnished bedroom and a bright yoga studio with wood floors share a hallway bathroom.

The garden is private and walled, with trees, flowering plants, outdoor seating, and a propane barbecue.

The neighborhood

Walk down the street and you reach the beach, with more beaches in both directions along the coast. Cafés, bakeries and restaurants are all within walking distance, from Japanese and sushi to grilled fish and traditional Portuguese food. The Farol Hotel bar is a good place for a drink at sunset.

Estoril is a few minutes away by car, and Lisbon is about 40 minutes by train from Cascais station.
